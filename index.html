THE IDEA

Free to learn. Built to explore.

Subash STEM Library is intended as a growing home for accessible STEM books and interactive resources. The architecture separates the books from the interactive learning objects so each can remain useful on its own.

The core educational material can remain freely accessible. Optional support and premium print editions can be added later without making the learning experience dependent on a paywall.
